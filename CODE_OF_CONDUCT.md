# Code of conduct

Be decent. If that needs elaborating: no harassment, no personal attacks, no
discrimination, and no treating a bug report as an accusation.

Technical disagreement is the point of a code repository and is not incivility. "This
is wrong and here is why" is welcome; "you are an idiot" is not. Assume the other person
has a reason you have not heard yet, and ask.

Two things specific to this project:

**Bug reports from events are the most valuable thing here, and often the least
polished.** Somebody typing a report at 23:00 after a bad twenty minutes at a door is
not going to produce a minimal reproduction. Meet that with gratitude, not a template.

**Security reports get patience.** Somebody who found a flaw and told us privately did
the right thing, at their own cost, and gets a thank you regardless of how it was
worded.

## If something goes wrong

Report it to the address on [github.com/ManasMadan](https://github.com/ManasMadan).
It will be read by one person, kept private, and acted on.
